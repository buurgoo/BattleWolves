# BattleWolves
Game description and development updates

## Introduction
BattleWolves is a turn-based fighter game with elements of the casual virtual pet genre. You raise your own wolf and send it to fight, gradually upgrading its room with the resources you earn from winning those fights.

## Game modes
The player has two in-game modes, each with different gameplay.

### Care mode
Take care of your wolf: feed it, brush its lush coat, fluff their pillows - they deserve it after the long full moon.

### Battle mode
Your wolf is ready to fight! Let it unleash its power.
